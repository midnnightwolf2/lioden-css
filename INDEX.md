## 🔍 Quick Reference

### Your Theme Colors
- **Gold:** #FFD700 (primary accent)
- **Firebrick Red:** #B22222 (backgrounds)
- **Dark Goldenrod:** #B8860B (secondary)
- **Off-White:** #F5F5F5 (text)

### Your Theme Fonts
- **Cinzel** - Body text
- **Cinzel Decorative** - Main headers
- **IM Fell English SC** - Section headers
- **UnifrakturMaguntia** - Gothic accents

### Key CSS Classes
- `.basic-box` - General content container
- `.pride-overview` - Pride intro with background
- `.queen-dynasty` / `.pride-dynasty` - Lineage sections
- `.alatus-box` - Special announcements
- `.comment-box` - Guestbook/comments
- `.roman-divider` - Section separator
- `.glow-gold` - Golden glow effect
- `.animated-glow` - Pulsing glow
